Before a refund is approved, you must allow our support team a reasonable opportunity to investigate and resolve any technical issues you are experiencing.

Refunds will not be approved for technical issues until our team has been given a fair chance to resolve them.

This process typically includes:

  1. Submitting a detailed description of the issue, including screenshots or screen recordings where applicable.
  2. Cooperating with our support team's troubleshooting steps (e.g., clearing cache, testing on a different device or browser, re-installing the app).
  3. Allowing up to 72 hours for our team to investigate and provide a resolution.

If the issue cannot be resolved within this period and is confirmed to be a platform-side bug that materially impairs your use of the Service, a refund may be approved.

4. Non-Refundable Cases

Refund requests will be automatically denied in the following circumstances:

  1. Renewal Charges: Refunds are not issued for any automatic subscription renewal. It is your responsibility to cancel before the renewal date. Cancellation instructions are available in Account Settings.
  2. Excessive Usage: If account logs indicate that you have substantially used the Service (e.g., completed more than 5 AI Tutor sessions or 3 curriculum modules during the refund window), this demonstrates meaningful consumption of the product, and a refund will not be granted.
  3. Rapid Purchase / Refund Cycles: Any pattern of subscribing, requesting a refund, and re-subscribing — regardless of the stated reason — will be treated as abuse of this policy. Such accounts may be permanently suspended without further refund eligibility.
  4. Misuse or Policy Violations: If your account has been suspended or terminated for violating our Terms of Service, you are not eligible for a refund.
  5. Change of Mind (After Window): Refund requests submitted after the applicable eligibility window, citing a change of mind or dissatisfaction, will not be honoured.
  6. Third-Party Purchases: Subscriptions purchased through third-party app stores (e.g., Apple App Store, Google Play Store) are subject to those platforms' own refund policies. ChirPolly has no ability to process refunds for such transactions — please contact the respective store directly.

Your account's access to all paid features will be immediately and automatically revoked upon confirmation of the refund.
