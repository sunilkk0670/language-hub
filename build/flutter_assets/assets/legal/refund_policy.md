# Refund & Cancellation Policy
**Entity:** Hrishikesh Innovations LLP
**Brand:** ChirPolly
**Last Updated:** February 2026

## 1. 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee
For all new subscribers, we offer a 7-day satisfaction guarantee. If you are unsatisfied with your premium experience, you may request a full refund within the first 7 days of your initial purchase.

## 2. Cancellation Policy
You may cancel your subscription at any time via the App Settings or by contacting our support team. Upon cancellation, your "Premium" access will remain active until the end of your current billing period. No further charges will be made.

## 3. Refund Eligibility & Exceptions
**Usage Limit:** To prevent abuse, refunds are only applicable if less than 20% of the premium modules (A2 and B1) have been consumed.

**Renewals:** This policy applies only to your initial purchase. Automatic monthly or annual renewals are not eligible for refunds once the new billing cycle has begun.

**Third-Party Fees:** In cases where government filing fees or specific third-party processing costs have already been incurred, those portions may be non-refundable.

## 4. Processing Times
Once approved, your refund will be credited back to your original payment method. Please allow 14 business days for the transaction to reflect in your bank statement.
